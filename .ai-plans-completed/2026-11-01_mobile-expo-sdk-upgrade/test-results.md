### Foreground-service type (E9): sources and decision
Android documentation read 2026-11-01 (timeout page and service-types page; quoted in plan amendment A2): at targetSdk 35 or higher, `dataSync` is capped at 6 hours per rolling 24 hours in the background and crashes the app if the service does not stop in `onTimeout`; `specialUse` has no stated cap but needs `FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE`, a manifest subtype property and a Play Console declaration. `react-native-background-actions` 4.1.0 implements `onTimeout` with `stopSelf` and maps `specialUse` (README type table, `BackgroundTaskOptions.java`). Owner decision: `specialUse` (amendment A2).
