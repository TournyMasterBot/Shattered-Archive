# Privacy Policy

## Overview

ShatteredArchive is an independent fan project that provides tooling and
research utilities for MUD players and enthusiasts. This document explains
how data is handled when using the ShatteredArchive Software and related
services.

---

## Data Collection

The ShatteredArchive Software does not intentionally collect personal
information by default.

Depending on configuration and usage, the software may process:

- User-generated gameplay logs
- Character names and in-game identifiers
- Connection details necessary to establish a MUD connection

All such data is derived from user interaction with the game and is
considered user-provided input.

---

## Data Storage

- Data generated by the software is stored locally by default.
- Server-side services operated by ShatteredArchive may temporarily process
  data to provide functionality, but are not intended to retain personal
  information beyond operational needs.

---

## Third-Party Services

ShatteredArchive may rely on third-party services (such as hosting providers
or dependency services) to operate infrastructure. These services operate
under their own privacy policies.

---

## Cookies and Tracking

The ShatteredArchive Software does not include built-in tracking, analytics,
or advertising components.

Public websites operated by ShatteredArchive may use minimal cookies or logs
necessary for basic operation.

---

## Changes

This privacy policy may be updated as the project evolves. Significant
changes will be documented in the repository.

---
