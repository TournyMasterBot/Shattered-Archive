==============================================================================
DSL2 CAPTURED GAME-ECHO PATTERNS — full community reference
==============================================================================
Generated 2026-07-15 for Dark and Shattered Lands (DSL), a MUD running on
Mudlet 4.20.1. Compiled by scanning:
  (1) DSL2's own .lua files (the active, in-use trigger set),
  (2) DSL2's own native Mudlet XML trigger tree (current/*.xml — objects
      built in the Trigger Editor, separate from the .lua layer),
  (3) the vendored PNP package source ("PNP files/", 46 files — DSL's
      long-standing community client, the origin of most of DSL2's own
      patterns),
  (4) sibling Mudlet profiles on the same machine (other DSL clients/
      characters) for anything not already covered above.

Patterns are Mudlet's PCRE regex dialect unless marked "(literal/substring)"
— those match as plain text, not regex, in the native Trigger Editor.

Sections marked **[ACTIVE — DSL2]** are live in DSL2 today (source: its own
.lua files). Sections marked **[NATIVE ONLY]** exist as native Mudlet
Trigger Editor objects in this profile (or a sibling) but have NOT been
ported into DSL2's own MyDSL_*.lua capture layer — real, confirmed DSL text,
but not currently driving any MyDSL window/state. Sections marked **[PNP
SOURCE]** are from the community PNP package, included for completeness/
cross-reference even where DSL2 built its own independent implementation.

==============================================================================
1. WEAPON-FLAG PROCS
==============================================================================
One-letter flag codes attribute a proc to the most recent attacker/target/
weapon-noun. **[ACTIVE — DSL2]** (MyDSL_DataLayer.lua ~3349-3439), ported
from and confirmed identical to **[PNP SOURCE]** (DSL_PNP_Battle.lua's
make_triggers(), Flag Trigger table) except where noted.

C — Frost:
  ^([\w\-\s,'"]+) freezes ([\w\-\s,'"]+)\.$
  ^The cold touch of ([\w\-\s,']+) surrounds you with ice

F — Flaming:
  ^([\w\-\s,']+) is burned by ([\w\-\s,']+)\.$
  ^([\w\-\s,']+) sears your flesh

L — Shocking:
  ^([\w\-\s,']+) is struck by lightning from ([\w\-\s,']+)\.$
  ^([\w\-\s,']+) is shocked by a

H — Vampiric:
  ^([\w\-\s,'"]+) draws life from ([\w\-\s,'"]+)\.$
  ^You feel ([\w\-\s,']+) drawing your life away

S — Stunning:
  ^([\w\-\s,'"]+) is knocked to the ground by ([\w\-\s,'"]+)\.$

M — Mana drain:
  ^You feel something drawing your energy away          (self; zero corpus occurrences confirmed yet)
  ^([\w\-\s,']+) draws energy from ([\w\-\s,']+)\.$

O — Holy:
  ^You feel a surge of ([\w\-\s,']+)'s holy wrath race through your body
  ^A flash of holy power erupts from ([\w\-\s,']+) and hits ([\w\-\s,']+)!$

U — Unholy:
  ^You feel a surge of ([\w\-\s,']+)'s unholy wrath race through your body   (zero corpus occurrences confirmed yet)

P — Poison (DSL2's own addition — no PNP equivalent, confirmed via sibling-profile logs):
  ^([\w\-\s,']+) coats ([\w\-\s,']+) with deadly lifebane poison\.$
  ^([\w\-\s,']+) is poisoned by the venom on ([\w\-\s,']+)\.$
  ^([\w\-\s,']+) shivers and suffers\.$

Confirmed non-functional / no echo at all:
  Sharp   — adds bonus damage silently, never echoes anything
  Vorpal  — confirmed non-functional, produces no echo

Damage (unified, one trigger for every damage verb):
  ^(You|[\w\-\s,']+?)(?:(?<=You)r|'s)?(?:\s?((?<=Your )[\w\s]+?|(?<='s )[\w\s]+?|))(?: do[es]*| [\>\<\=\*]+|) (miss|scratch|graze|hit|injure|wound|maul|decimate|devastate|maim|MUTILATE|DISEMBOWEL|DISMEMBER|MASSACRE|MANGLE|DEMOLISH|DEVASTATE|OBLITERATE|ANNIHILATE|ERADICATE|GHASTLY|HORRID|DREADFUL|HIDEOUS|INDESCRIBABLE|UNSPEAKABLE)[esES]*(?: things to| [\>\<\=\*]+|) ([\w\-\s,']+)([\.\.!]+)$
  Strictly anchored to the attacker name — deliberately excludes Coliseum
  broadcast-prefixed lines (own future AGL/Coliseum module territory).

Avoidance (direct port of PNP's DSL_PNP_Battle.lua):
  (You|[\w\-\,\s']+) (dodge)s? (your|[\w\-\,\s']+) attack\.$
  (You|[\w\-\,\s']+) (parry|parries) (your|[\w\-\,\s']+) attack\.$
  (You|[\w\-\,\s']+) (block)[s]? (your|[\w\-\,\s']+) attack .*\.$
  ^[\w\-\s,']+ senses they.?re about to be hit and deflects the blow\.        (bard-only, exact wording unconfirmed live yet)
  ^[\w\-\s,']+ senses [\w\-\s,']+'s attack coming and avoids its blow\.       (same caveat)

Condition (8 tiers total — 7 HP tiers + a distinct mana tier, confirmed via
DSL2's own native XML "Health/Mana" trigger group; DSL2's active .lua
pattern uses shorter substrings than PNP's full sentences, which still
match fine since the pattern isn't end-anchored — full PNP wording shown
alongside for accuracy):
  Active pattern (self+third-person forms merged):
    (?:is in excellent condition|are in excellent condition|has a few scratches|have a few scratches|has some small wounds|have some small wounds|has some big nasty wounds|have some big nasty wounds|has quite a few wounds|have quite a few wounds|looks pretty hurt|look pretty hurt|is in awful condition|are in awful condition)
  Full literal PNP/native wording (excellent → awful):
    "is in excellent condition."
    "has a few scratches."
    "has some small wounds and bruises."      <- DSL2's active pattern omits "and bruises"
    "has quite a few wounds."
    "has some big nasty wounds and scratches." <- DSL2's active pattern omits "and scratches"
    "looks pretty hurt."
    "is in awful condition."
  Mana condition tier **[NATIVE ONLY]** — not yet captured by DSL2's own
  condition parser at all:
    "full magical ability."

Death (two forms — second has no PNP equivalent):
   is DEAD!!$
   hits the ground \.\.\. DEAD\.$
  **[UNCONFIRMED]** third form, found in sibling profiles, not in DSL2's
  own .lua or native XML:
    "You hear something's death cry."

-- **[ACTIVE — DSL2]** (MyDSL_ChatTriggers.lua) — all patterns anchored to
   line start ("^\a?...", the \a is a real optional BEL control char DSL
   sometimes prefixes) and use "[^']+"/"[^']*" prefixes (stop at the first
   quote) to prevent one message's quoted dialogue text from cross-matching
   a different channel's verb. gag=false means the line still echoes to
   main console too. --

Tells (gag=false):
  ^\a?You tell [^']*']
  ^\a?[^']+ tells you[^']*']

Group chat:
  ^\a?[^']+ tells the group[^']*']
  ^\a?You tell the group[^']*']

OOC (direct):
  ^\a?[^']+ OOC:[^']*']

City (gossip family):
  ^\a?[^']+ (?:clan )?gossips[^']*']
  ^\a?(?:[^']+ )?(?:OOC )?Kingdom: ']

  ^\a?(?:You say|[^']+ says)[^']*']              gag=false
  ^\a?(?:You whisper|[^']+ whispers)[^']*']
  ^\a?(?:You yell|[^']+ yells)[^']*']            gag=false
  ^\a?(?:You shout|[^']+ shouts)[^']*']          gag=false

  ^\a?[^']+ grats[^']*']
